Strategic Content Partnerships Plan

Goal: Turn trusted creators and communities into distribution surfaces for clarity, not ads
North Star: Credibility → Context → Quiet conversion

1. Partnership Philosophy (Lock This In)

We are not buying attention.
We are embedding analysis into places where decisions already happen.

Our role in every partnership:

Analyst

Data contributor

Sanity-check layer

Regret-prevention lens

Hard rules:

No affiliate links

No “sponsored by”

No CTA in the content itself

OFFO mentioned only as source, not product

2. Primary Channel: EV YouTube Creators (Used EV Focus)
Target Profile (Very Specific)

You are not looking for:

EV hype channels

First-drive reviewers

“Top 10 EVs” content

You want creators who:

Review used EVs

Talk about ownership tradeoffs

Cover battery health, depreciation, real-life use

Have trust, not massive reach (5K–100K subs is ideal)

Examples of segment types (not creators):

“Is this used Model 3 still worth it?”

“Living with a used Leaf in 2025”

“What I’d check before buying this EV”

Partnership Offer (Exact Pitch Framing)

What you offer:

A free, independent analysis of the specific EV they’re reviewing

Clear risks, fit conditions, and failure modes

Plain English, not marketing

What you do NOT offer:

Payment

Scripts

Talking points

Endorsements

Outreach Pitch (Template)

I’m not selling anything — I work on EV decision analysis.

If you’re reviewing a used EV, I can run an independent fit + risk analysis on that exact model/listing and send you a one-page summary you can reference in your video.

Totally free. If it’s useful, feel free to cite it as “analysis courtesy of OFFO Labs.” If not, no worries at all.

This framing:

Removes pressure

Signals confidence

Makes them curious

Keeps control with the creator

How It Appears in Their Content

Acceptable mentions:

“We had an independent analysis done…”

“According to OFFO Labs’ analysis…”

“Data provided by OFFO Labs”

Not acceptable:

“Check out this tool”

“Sponsored by”

Links in description unless they ask

Your win is:

Spoken credibility

Screenshot of analysis

Source attribution

Internal Workflow (So You Don’t Burn Out)

Creator agrees → sends model/listing

You generate:

30-second summary

“What breaks first”

Fit conditions

Deliver as:

1-page PDF

Or shareable report link (anonymous)

Archive it internally as:

“Creator Analysis – Model X – Date”

Later, these become:

Case studies

Pattern libraries

Landing-page proof

3. Secondary Channel: Guest Writing (Blogs / Newsletters)
Target Publications

Not mainstream EV news.

Look for:

Niche EV newsletters

Used-car buyer newsletters

Energy / sustainability blogs with thoughtful audiences

Apartment-living / urban mobility blogs

Audience should already be thinking, not browsing.

Content Angle (Do Not Pitch Product)

You pitch ideas, not tools.

Example Article Titles

“Why Range Anxiety Is the Wrong Question for Used EV Buyers”

“The Hidden Cost of Unpredictable Charging”

“When an EV Is the Right Car — Just Not for You”

Your role:

Data contributor / analyst

Bio line:

Contributor to OFFO Labs, which studies EV–lifestyle fit and ownership risk.

No CTA.
No signup push.

Why This Works

Editors want insight, not ads

You become a cited source

Readers self-select later when they’re unsure

This builds slow trust, which matches your product.

4. Community Wikis & Forums (Long-Tail Authority)
Target Surfaces

EV model-specific forums

Owner wikis

Community-maintained guides

Enthusiast documentation

Examples of contribution types:

“Used EV Buying Checklist”

“Charging Predictability Considerations”

“Apartment Ownership Caveats”

How to Contribute (Important)

You do NOT link to a tool.

You:

Share frameworks

Share decision logic

Share failure modes

Attribution line:

Analysis framework contributed by OFFO Labs

This creates:

Durable authority

Search surface

Long-term discovery

5. Measurement (What Success Actually Looks Like)

Do not track success as:

Clicks

Signups

Follower growth

Track:

How often OFFO is cited without prompting

DMs that start with “I saw you mentioned…”

People pasting listings and asking “does this fit?”

Early win benchmarks (realistic):

2–3 creator collaborations in 60 days

1 guest article published

3–5 unsolicited inbound questions

That’s a strong signal for this category.

6. Guardrails (Avoid This at All Costs)

🚫 No mass outreach
🚫 No “media kits”
🚫 No influencer marketplaces
🚫 No tracking pixels in shared reports
🚫 No retargeting

Anything that smells like growth hacking will destroy trust.

7. 30-Day Execution Plan
Week 1

Identify 10 creators (used EV focus)

Draft 1-page sample analysis (generic model)

Prepare outreach template

Week 2

Reach out to 5 creators (max)

Pitch guest article to 2 publications

Week 3

Deliver first free analysis

Observe what creators react to / question

Week 4

Refine analysis language

Document patterns

Decide whether to scale or pause

Final Framing (For You Internally)

This strategy is not about reach.
It’s about placing calm intelligence next to noisy decisions.

If done right:

OFFO becomes “the people who tell the uncomfortable truth”

Creators trust you

Users come to you already believing
