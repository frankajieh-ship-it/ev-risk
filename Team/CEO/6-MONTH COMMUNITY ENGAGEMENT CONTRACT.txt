6-MONTH COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT CONTRACT

Role: Community Intelligence & Reddit Engagement

CONTRACT AGREEMENT

Term: 6 months
Role: Community & Behavioral Research Contributor
Reports to: Product Owner / CEO
Status: Contractor (unless otherwise specified)

1. Scope of Work

The Contributor will:

Engage in relevant Reddit communities (EV, charging, used EVs, ownership)

Respond to posts and comments using OFFO-approved framing

Surface high-signal behavioral patterns

Capture recurring friction, mismatch, and regret signals

Escalate insights to Product / Content teams weekly

The Contributor is not responsible for:

Selling

Lead generation

Defending EVs

Evangelism

Arguing or persuading

2. Behavioral Guardrails (Non-Negotiable)

Contributor must:

Remain neutral, calm, and explanatory

Avoid recommendations (“you should buy”, “don’t buy”)

Avoid ideological or political framing

Avoid specs-first arguments

Always center routine fit and predictability

Contributor must always:

End Reddit replies with a follow-up question

Either:

invite discussion, or

reference a problem OFFO helps clarify (lightly, non-promotional)

3. Deliverables

Weekly:

10–20 high-quality Reddit engagements

1–2 surfaced patterns (with quotes)

1 short internal summary:

What confused people?

What surprised them?

What caused regret?

Monthly:

One synthesized insight memo

5. Termination

Immediate termination for:

NDA violation

Promotional or deceptive behavior

Misrepresentation of OFFO as a seller or authority
