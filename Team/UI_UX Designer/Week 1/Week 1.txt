Welcome to OFFO / EV Risk
It’s great to have you with us. You’re stepping into a role that doesn’t just build features—it builds trust.

Here’s What You Should Know on Day One
We are not a dashboard company.
We are a decision intelligence company. Our job is to help someone make a better, clearer choice when buying a used EV—a decision filled with financial and emotional stakes.

Our product’s success depends entirely on whether the user believes the system is thinking with them, not just processing their data.

Your Role in This Mission
As the UX / Content Systems Designer (Part-Time / Contract), you are not here to make things pretty.
You are here to design how the system explains itself.

Your Core Responsibility:
Design the voice of intelligence.
Every word, every explanation, every prompt must feel:

Calm — never frantic or salesy

Clear — never clever or ambiguous

Honest — never overconfident or misleading

What That Means in Practice:
You will own:

Report language templates

“Why this matters” explanations

Trust-calibration copy (e.g., “We don’t know this yet, but here’s why it matters”)

Microcopy for personalization flows

The narrative flow from zero-data to personalized insight

You will NOT own:

Visual design, colors, or UI polish

Marketing or promotional copy

Feature concepts in isolation

How to Start Thinking Like Us
Before you write a single sentence, ask:
Does this reduce uncertainty?
If it doesn’t help the user understand the risk or the recommendation better, it’s noise.

Does this make the system feel more honest?
Admitting uncertainty builds more trust than pretending to know everything.

Does this respect the user’s time and intelligence?
We ask for personalization only when it meaningfully improves confidence—and we explain why.

Your North Star:
Good copy here is not persuasive—it is clarifying.
If it feels like marketing, it’s wrong. If it feels like a thoughtful expert explaining something complex simply, it’s right.

Your First 30–60 Days
First 30 Days:
Immerse yourself in the existing report flows and language

Identify where users likely feel doubt, confusion, or friction

Rewrite one key report section to be clearer and build more trust

Partner with the Product Engineer to understand the confidence model

By 60 Days:
Establish a consistent, calm, intelligent voice across the product

Improve personalization opt-in rates through better explanation

Reduce “Why are you asking this?” friction without adding more text

How We’ll Measure Your Success
Drop-off rates decrease at key explanation points

Support tickets about confusion drop

Personalization opt-in rates increase

User feedback indicates higher perceived clarity and trust

A Final Note
You are not here to make the product sound smart.
You are here to make it feel trustworthy.

If a user finishes a report and thinks:
“This system understands the problem—and respects me.”

Then you’ve done your job.

We’re excited to have you shape this with us. Let’s build something that matters.

📋 Weekly Tasks
1. Deep Dive & Audit (Days 1-2)
Review 3 existing EV Risk reports (example cases provided)

Where explanations feel technical vs. accessible

Where the system "asks" vs. "explains why it's asking"

2. Trust Calibration Analysis (Day 3)
Identify 3-5 "confidence statements" in reports that need calibration

Propose rewrites that:

Acknowledge uncertainty where appropriate

Explain inference logic simply

Maintain authority without arrogance

3. Personalization Prompt Review (Day 4)
Audit all personalization prompts (where we ask for user input)

Score each on clarity:

"Why are we asking this?" (explanation quality)

"What changes if I answer?" (value clarity)

"Can I skip this?" (permission clarity)

Flag top 2 prompts for immediate improvement

4. Microcopy Framework (Day 5)
Draft a "Content Principles" one-pager including:

Our voice attributes (calm, clear, honest)

Sentence structure guidelines

Uncertainty communication framework

"What not to do" examples

Share with Product Engineer & CEO for alignment
