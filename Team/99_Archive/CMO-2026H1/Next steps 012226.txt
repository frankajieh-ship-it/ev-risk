As Lead Marketing & Content Manager, my recommended next steps are about sequencing and discipline, not doing more at once. You’re in a strong position — the key is to compound clarity.

1. Lock the Content Spine (This Week)

Before publishing anything else, formally lock this as your core editorial spine:

The EV Ownership Reality Curve
Months 0–3 → Months 4–12 → Year 1+

Everything you publish for the next 8–12 weeks should map to one of these phases.

Why this matters

Prevents topic drift

Makes OFFO feel intentional, not reactive

Lets readers self-identify (“I’m in this phase”)

Action

Create an internal tag system: PHASE_1, PHASE_2, PHASE_3

Every post must declare its phase before writing

2. Publish Phase 1 Anchor Post (Week 1)

Start with Months 0–3, because:

It’s emotionally safe

It resonates with both buyers and owners

It doesn’t trigger defensiveness

Primary blog (already outlined):

“The First 90 Days of EV Ownership: Why Everyone Charges Too Early at First”

Distribution

Publish on OFFO blog

Share as:

a Reddit-native long comment (not a link post)

a short reflection post in r/EVRoutine

Goal

Establish OFFO as “the people who understand early EV anxiety without hype”

3. Run Reddit as a Listening Engine, Not a Megaphone (Weeks 1–4)

For the next month:

Do

Comment using phase language:

“That’s very Month 2 behavior — totally normal.”

“This is usually where Month 6 friction shows up.”

Ask reflective questions tied to phases:

“Did this fade for you after a few months, or did it stick?”

Do NOT

Link the blog unless someone asks

Introduce the tool unless uncertainty is explicit

Why
You are seeding language, not clicks.

4. Phase 2 Content Is the Conversion Engine (Weeks 3–6)

Once Phase 1 is live and circulating, move to Months 4–12.

This is where:

regret begins quietly

planning tolerance is tested

winter / disruption shows up

Key Phase 2 post

“If an EV Still Feels High-Maintenance After 6 Months, Here’s Why”

This is your highest-intent content.

Tool intro rule (important)

Only introduce OFFO here

Only after naming friction

Always with permission

Example:

“If you want structure to tell whether this is temporary or structural, we’ve built a short routine check.”

5. Build One Canonical Page (Quietly)

Create one evergreen page on the site:

Title

“How EV Ownership Actually Evolves Over Time”

This page:

summarizes the 3 phases

has no CTA at the top

links to each phase blog

becomes your “explain OFFO” destination

This is what you link when asked.

6. Start Capturing “Phase Language Reuse” (Metric Shift)

Begin tracking a new qualitative metric:

Are users repeating OFFO’s framing unprompted?

Examples:

“This feels like setup anxiety”

“It technically works but it’s mentally expensive”

“Charging hasn’t faded into the background”

This is more important than traffic.

7. Defer Everything Else (Very Important)

For now, explicitly do not:

launch email capture

expand to new platforms

optimize SEO

push monetization

add new content themes

You are still teaching the market how to think.

Executive Content North Star

If every piece of content makes someone think:

“Oh… that explains what I’ve been feeling.”

You’re winning.

If it makes them think:

“Is this telling me what to buy?”

You’re off-strategy.
