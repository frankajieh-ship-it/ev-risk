**PHASE 1 — IMMERSION (Week 1)**

Objective



Internalize OFFO’s language, tone, and framing so you sound native, not branded.



Required Reading (Daily)



Read without commenting for the first 3–4 days.



Focus subs:



r/electricvehicles



r/EVs



r/teslamotors (observe, don’t engage early)



r/EVRoutine (read everything)



What to Study (Checklist)



For each thread, note:



What triggered replies?



Where did frustration appear?



What made people open up?



Where did arguments start?



Which comments calmed the thread?



Create a simple doc with:



“Common Frictions”



“Language People Use”



“Moments of Uncertainty”







PHASE 2 — LANGUAGE CALIBRATION (Week 1–2)

Core OFFO Language (Must Memorize)



These phrases are approved anchors:



“This mostly works, except…”



“It’s not range — it’s predictability”



“This depends more on routine than the car”



“This works until something changes”



“That’s a fit issue, not a tech failure”



Language to Avoid (Hard No)



Never use:



“Best EV”



“Perfect”



“You should”



“Solution”



“Fix”



“Upgrade”



“Game changer”



Any CTA language







PHASE 3 — COMMENTING FRAMEWORK (Week 2)

The 3-Step Comment Structure (MANDATORY)



Every comment should follow this shape:



1\. Validate (1 sentence)



Acknowledge their experience without agreeing or disagreeing.



“That frustration is real — especially once you’re already standing at the charger.”



2\. Reframe (1–2 sentences)



Shift from surface complaint → underlying friction.



“What makes it stressful isn’t the charger count, it’s not knowing which path will work this time.”



3\. Open (1 sentence)



Invite reflection, not response.



“Does this show up more when your schedule changes or when you’re traveling?”



Stop.

Do not conclude.

Do not advise unless asked.







PHASE 4 — REDIRECTING TO r/EVRoutine (Critical Skill)

When You Are Allowed to Redirect



Only redirect when:



User shares personal constraints



User says “I wish I knew earlier”



User asks “Does this work for people like me?”



Thread is already deep (not top-level drive-by)



How to Redirect (Exact Pattern)



Never say “join our community.”



Say:



“This kind of ‘mostly works except…’ discussion is exactly what people have been sharing in r/EVRoutine — it’s focused on lived setups, not specs.”



Then stop.



No links unless asked.

No follow-up push.



PHASE 5 — ENGAGING WITH MAIN ACCOUNT POSTS

Your Role Under Main Account Threads



You are not a hype amplifier.



You:



respond to commenters



deepen nuance



ask clarifying questions



surface edge cases



Goal



Turn:



“Interesting post”



into:



“This is exactly my situation…”



Example Reply



“This tends to show up once routines stabilize — especially if charging access isn’t fully in your control. Did anything change after the first few months?”



This creates self-identification, not clicks.



DAILY OPERATING CHECKLIST



Each day, aim for:



5–10 thoughtful comment replies



0 promotional posts



1–2 gentle r/EVRoutine mentions (only when appropriate)



0 links dropped unless explicitly requested



METRICS YOU TRACK (Not Numbers)



End each day, answer:



Did someone reuse OFFO language?



Did someone share a personal constraint?



Did someone ask a situational question?



Did a thread calm down after my reply?



If yes to 1–2 → good day.



RED FLAGS (Escalate Immediately)



Stop and flag to founder if:



Someone accuses shilling



A thread turns ideological



You feel tempted to “correct” someone



You feel pressure to post more



MENTAL MODEL TO HOLD



You are not here to win arguments.

You are here to give people words.



If people leave a thread thinking:



“Oh — this isn’t about range.”



You did your job.
