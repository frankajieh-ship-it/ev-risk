STUDY & TRAINING MATERIALS

MANDATORY BEFORE FIRST POST

This is how you prevent off-brand replies and accidental harm.

A. Core Mental Model (Must Memorize)

EVs don’t fail on specs.
They fail when routines don’t fit.

OFFO exists to:

reduce mental overhead

surface mismatch early

explain tradeoffs calmly

B. Language to USE (Approved)

mental overhead

routine fit

planning friction

predictability > availability

behavioral mismatch

“this works when…”

“this becomes frustrating if…”

C. Language to AVOID (Never Use)

“good / bad EV”

“range anxiety”

“just plan better”

“you should buy”

“EVs are better”

“this is a dealbreaker”

D. Reddit Reply Structure (Standard)

Acknowledge the user’s experience

Reframe in behavioral terms

Explain why the friction happens

Normalize their decision

Ask one follow-up question

Optional: soft invite to r/EVRoutine

E. Required Follow-Up Question Rule

Every reply must end with either:

A clarifying question about routine

OR a question inviting others to share experience

OR a question about what mattered most in the decision

Never end with a statement.

F. Canonical Example Sentence (Gold Standard)

“Because you report regular long-distance trips, this behavior would likely show up a few times per year and add planning friction at charging stops.”

This sentence is approved for reuse and adaptation.

G. What Success Looks Like

Users feel understood, not corrected

Threads stay civil

People explain why EVs didn’t fit — not just that they didn’t

OFFO is perceived as a neutral thinking partner

H. What Failure Looks Like

Arguments

Spec debates

Defensive tone

Promotion

“EV vs ICE” framing

Sounding like a dealership or blog

Final Internal Note (for you)

This role is not social media.
It is behavioral intelligence collection and trust-building.
