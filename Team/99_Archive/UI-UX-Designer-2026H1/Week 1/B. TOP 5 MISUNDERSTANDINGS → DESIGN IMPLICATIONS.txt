Here's how these translate to immediate UI/UX changes:

1. "More chargers = less anxiety"
Design Fix:

Never show charger counts without context

Instead: "Charging predictability" visualization

jsx
// UI Component: Charging Predictability Indicator
<ChargingContext>
  <PredictabilityIndicator 
    value={user_has_dedicated_home_charging ? "High" : "Variable"}
    explanation={
      user_has_dedicated_home_charging 
      ? "Charging fits naturally into your routine"
      : "Charging requires active planning each week"
    }
  />
</ChargingContext>
2. "Range anxiety = daily concern"
Design Fix:

Distinguish between routine vs edge-case anxiety

Visualize: "Your typical week" vs "Your occasional trips"

jsx
// Component: Range Contextualizer
<RangeAnalysis>
  <TypicalUse>
    <Checkmark /> Covers 95% of your weekly driving
    <Note>40-mile commute fits easily</Note>
  </TypicalUse>
  <EdgeCase>
    <Warning /> Requires planning for occasional trips
    <Note>Your 2x/year 200-mile trip needs charging stop</Note>
  </EdgeCase>
</RangeAnalysis>
3. "EV vs ICE is binary"
Design Fix:

Comparative framing without comparison

Show: "How EV ownership changes your routine"

jsx
// Component: Routine Shift Explanation
<RoutineShift>
  <Heading>How this changes your refueling habit:</Heading>
  <Comparison>
    <ICE>Gas station every 7-10 days (5-10 min)</ICE>
    <EV>Plug in every 2-3 days (30 sec at home)</EV>
  </Comparison>
  <Insight>
    Less time spent, but requires consistent access
  </Insight>
</RoutineShift>
4. "Fast charging solves everything"
Design Fix:

Surface the mental overhead of DC reliance

Visualize: "Planning burden" vs "convenience"

jsx
// Component: Charging Type Burden
<ChargingBurden>
  <PrimaryMethod method={user.primary_charging}>
    {user.primary_charging === 'dc_fast' ? (
      <Warning>
        <Icon>⏰</Icon>
        Requires 30-45 min planning blocks weekly
      </Warning>
    ) : (
      <Success>
        <Icon>🏠</Icon>
        Fits into existing home routines
      </Success>
    )}
  </PrimaryMethod>
</ChargingBurden>
5. "Battery % = certainty"
Design Fix:

Never show battery health as standalone number

Always contextualize: "What this means for your planning"

jsx
// Component: Battery Health Translator
<BatteryContext>
  <HealthValue value={battery_health_percentage}>
    {battery_health_percentage}% estimated capacity
  </HealthValue>
  <PracticalImplication>
    <PlanningImpact>
      Adds {calculate_buffer_needed(battery_health_percentage)} 
      miles of planning buffer to your routine
    </PlanningImpact>
    <MentalLoad>
      {battery_health_percentage > 85 ? "Low" : "Moderate"} 
      additional planning effort
    </MentalLoad>
  </PracticalImplication>
</BatteryContext>
