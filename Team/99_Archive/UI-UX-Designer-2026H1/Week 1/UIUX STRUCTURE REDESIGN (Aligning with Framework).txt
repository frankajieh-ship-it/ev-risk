1. Information Hierarchy Redesign
Based on Content Principles v1.0, here's the new report structure:

jsx
// Report Structure (Top to Bottom)
<EVRiskReport>
  {/* TIER 1: Safety & Immediate Reliability */}
  <SafetySection>
    <Recalls />
    <StrandingRisks />
    <SafetyCriticalIssues />
  </SafetySection>

  {/* TIER 2: Battery & Long-term Planning */}
  <BatterySection>
    <BehavioralExplanation>
      How battery uncertainty affects your weekly planning
    </BehavioralExplanation>
    <LongTermImplications>
      What this means for ownership duration
    </LongTermImplications>
  </BatterySection>

  {/* TIER 3: Routine Fit */}
  <RoutineFitSection>
    <ChargingPredictability>
      How charging fits (or doesn't) into your routine
    </ChargingPredictability>
    <MentalOverloadIndicators>
      Where additional planning is required
    </MentalOverloadIndicators>
  </RoutineFitSection>

  {/* TIER 4: Cost & Convenience */}
  <CostSection>
    <FinancialExposure />
    <ConvenienceTradeoffs />
  </CostSection>
</EVRiskReport>
2. Confidence Display Framework
Problem: Current design might imply judgment
Solution: Confidence as "explanation quality" not "vehicle quality"

jsx
// New Confidence Component
<ConfidenceExplanation>
  <Heading>How well we can explain this EV's fit</Heading>
  
  <ConfidenceLevel level={confidence}>
    {/* Visual: Not a score, but a completeness indicator */}
    <CompletenessIndicator>
      <Segment filled={has_battery_data} />
      <Segment filled={has_charging_context} />
      <Segment filled={has_usage_pattern} />
      <Segment filled={has_climate_data} />
    </CompletenessIndicator>
    
    <Explanation>
      {confidence === 'high' && "We have enough information to explain how this EV fits into daily life"}
      {confidence === 'medium' && "We can explain general patterns, but personal context would clarify"}
      {confidence === 'low' && "Key information is missing, making specific guidance limited"}
    </Explanation>
    
    <MissingData>
      {!has_battery_data && "• Battery health data"}
      {!has_charging_context && "• Your charging situation"}
    </MissingData>
  </ConfidenceLevel>
</ConfidenceExplanation>
3. Personalization Request Redesign
Current risk: Feels like registration
New approach: Inline, value-driven, optional

jsx
// Inline Personalization Request
<InlineContextRequest>
  <Trigger>Want more specific guidance?</Trigger>
  
  <ValueProposition>
    <Bullet>Share your commute → See exact charging frequency</Bullet>
    <Bullet>Add your parking → Understand planning burden</Bullet>
    <Bullet>Skip → Get general patterns</Bullet>
  </ValueProposition>
  
  <QuickInputs>
    <SingleInput 
      question="Typical weekly miles?" 
      options={["Under 100", "100-200", "200+"]}
      onAnswer={updateAnalysis}
    />
  </QuickInputs>
  
  <SkipOption>Skip for now → Show general patterns</SkipOption>
</InlineContextRequest>
