# Research Focus Areas
1. **Apartment Dwellers** (highest anxiety)
   - How do they describe charging planning?
   - What surprises them post-purchase?
   - Language they use for "predictability"

2. **First-time EV Buyers** 
   - What assumptions were wrong?
   - What mental load wasn't anticipated?
   - When do they feel regret vs satisfaction?

3. **Cold Climate Owners**
   - How does winter change routine?
   - What planning adjustments are needed?
   - Language for "buffer" and "margin"
