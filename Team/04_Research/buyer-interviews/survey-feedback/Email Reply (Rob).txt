  If someone has reliable home charging, schedule control, and backup options, the report explicitly names that stability instead of implying friction that isn’t there.

* **Schedule questions were clarified**
  We split “predictability” into *schedule variability* vs *schedule control*, which fixes the retired / self-directed schedule mismatch you called out.

* **Climate framing was corrected**
  We no longer label regions as “favorable” in a blanket way. Instead, we describe conditions (cold winters, hot summers) and pair them with the user’s stated tolerance.

* **Vehicle details are now clearly contextual**
  They no longer imply that the result is judging the car — the outcome is anchored to routine fit, as intended.

* **Seasonal sensitivity is handled explicitly**
  Not as generic range loss, but as “does this resurface mentally in certain months, or fade into the background?”

Your point about software maturity and tolerance also landed — that’s now on our roadmap as its own friction axis, because you’re right: for some owners it’s background noise, for others it’s a real source of stress.
