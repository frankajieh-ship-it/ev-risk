1️⃣ Phase 0.5 — “What’s Missing / Why It Matters / Confidence”
Dimension	Assessment
Behavioral problem addressed	Users experience uncertainty and anxiety when key decision inputs are missing but cannot tell which gaps matter most.
Mental overhead reduced	Reduces unstructured worry by explicitly naming unknowns and explaining their impact. Prevents users from mentally “filling gaps” with worst-case assumptions.
Predictability clarified	Clarifies how confident the system can be given current information and what would change that confidence. Predictability of guidance support improves, not outcome predictability.
Failure modes surfaced	✔ Missing battery data
✔ Unknown charging context
✔ Typical used-EV information gaps
Notes

Strength: Excellent trust calibration; works even for zero-data users.

Risk: If overused later, could feel repetitive — should remain an entry layer, not repeated downstream.

Verdict: Well-aligned. Foundational. No change needed.

2️⃣ Battery Health Block
Dimension	Assessment
Behavioral problem addressed	Users anchor on mileage or age and misjudge long-term risk when battery condition is uncertain or poorly explained.
Mental overhead reduced	Reduces anxiety caused by abstract battery metrics by translating uncertainty into planning implications (e.g., range buffer, resale, longevity).
Predictability clarified	Clarifies predictability of long-term ownership assumptions (replacement horizon, degradation expectations).
Failure modes surfaced	✔ Missing battery health data
✔ Degraded battery + long-term ownership
✔ Battery uncertainty amplifying other stresses
Notes

Strength: High salience; users intuitively care about this.

Gap: Needs consistent framing that battery uncertainty amplifies routine stress, not just cost or range.

Overreach risk: None, as long as it avoids predicting failure or lifespan.

Verdict: Correctly standalone. Needs language alignment, not structural change.

3️⃣ Charging Fit Block (Mental Overhead / Routine Friction)
Dimension	Assessment
Behavioral problem addressed	Users underestimate how charging predictability affects daily planning burden and long-term satisfaction.
Mental overhead reduced	Reduces repeated micro-decisions around when and where to charge by explaining routine friction patterns.
Predictability clarified	Clarifies predictability of charging as a habit (background vs foreground task).
Failure modes surfaced	✔ Apartment/shared charging unpredictability
✔ DC fast charging as primary fuel
✔ Lack of nearby backup options
Notes

Strength: Directly operationalizes the “mental overhead” insight.

Gap: Needs clearer reference to how battery uncertainty multiplies charging stress.

Overreach risk: Must avoid drifting into recommendations or scoring.

Verdict: Strong but incomplete without cross-reference to battery certainty.
